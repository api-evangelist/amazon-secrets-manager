This technical guide demonstrates two features of the AWS Workload Credentials Provider: role chaining for accessing secrets across AWS accounts, and prefetching capabilities that retrieve and cache secrets locally to reduce cold-start latency, improve availability during transient failures, and lower costs. It covers explicit secret prefetching and tag-based discovery configuration.
